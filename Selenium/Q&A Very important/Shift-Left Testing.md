#Shift-Left Testing:
Shift-left testing is a software development approach that moves testing activities earlier in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), 
shifting them "left" on a traditional project timeline. Instead of waiting until coding or integration is finished, testing begins during the requirements, planning, 
and design phases.


Shift Left Testing is a software testing approach where testing activities are started early in the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC). 
It helps teams identify and fix defects during the initial stages of development, reducing cost and improving software quality.

Testing begins from the requirement and design phase.
Early bug detection reduces development time and cost.
Improves collaboration between developers, testers, and business teams.
