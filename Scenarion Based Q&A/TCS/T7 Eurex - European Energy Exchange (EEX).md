#Eurex, the European Energy Exchange (EEX), Xetra, and the Nodal Exchange : 
Are major financial and commodity trading venues that all run on the T7 trading architecture, a high-speed electronic trading technology developed by the 
 Deutsche Börse Group . [1]  Overview of Each Venue 

***• Eurex: Eur-European  e-Energy  x-Exchange
		One of the world's largest  derivatives exchanges  specializing in futures and options (especially European fixed-income and equity indices). 
***• European Energy Exchange (EEX): 
		The leading energy exchange in Europe, handling contracts for power, natural gas, and emission allowances. 

***• Xetra (Xetra stands: Exchange Electronic Trading ): - Frankfurt Stock Exchange operates the Xetra platform.
		A premier fully electronic cash market trading venue primarily used for trading equities and exchange-traded products (ETPs) in Germany and across Europe. 
***• Nodal Exchange: 
		A US-based regulated derivatives exchange offering electric power, natural gas, and environmental contracts in North America (part of the EEX Group). [7, 8]
